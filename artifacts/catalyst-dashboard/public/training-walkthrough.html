Chapter 1

Before you start

Make sure at least one person per observation group has cross-school access to Catalyst. Email catalyst@uncommonschools.org and notify the facilitator if this is not the case.

Chapter 2

Scoring the school

For school-wide rubrics — scored once for the building, from the cross-regional dashboard rather than a school’s own page.

  1. Go to the cross-regional rollup. If you are inside a school, the ← Network link at the top takes you back out.
  2. Set the rubric picker to the school-wide rubric. The grid switches to one row per school.
  3. Click + Add School Observation.
  4. Choose the school, set the date, score each domain, add the team’s notes.
  5. Mark it as a walkthrough, and submit.

The + Add School Observation button only appears for network-level accounts, and only when a school-wide rubric is selected. If you cannot see it, check the rubric picker.

No teacher, no rescore

A school-wide score belongs to the building, so there is nobody to send back and rescore. It will not appear in the rescore queue. Only teacher-level rubrics produce rescores.

Chapter 3

Scoring a teacher

Classroom observations — one per teacher observed, from the school’s own Dashboard.

Observation Steps

  1. Observe the class as a team, using the paper trackers provided.
  2. Step out into the hallway five minutes before the observation window ends.
  3. In the hallway, align on the scores — one person enters them into Catalyst.
  4. Align on the Glows and Grows — the same person enters the feedback.
  5. Do not record an action step.

Entering it in Catalyst

  1. From the cross-regional rollup, click into the school.
  2. Set the rubric picker to the rubric you are using.
  3. Click + Add Observation and choose the teacher.
  4. Set the date, time, and course.
  5. Turn the Walkthrough toggle on. Bottom row of the form, next to Close and Submit. It is off by default.
  6. Score each domain 0, 0.5, or 1.
  7. Add the team’s Glows and Grows, but do not record an action step.
  8. Leave Email Teacher Feedback off — do not send teacher feedback emails from a walkthrough.
  9. Submit, and repeat for the next teacher.
Check the toggle

Ensure the Walkthrough toggle is ON, so that it records an official walkthrough observation. It is off by default.

Chapter 4

On your phone

Go to catalyst.uncommonschools.org on your phone and Catalyst takes you to the mobile site automatically — same address, same sign-in, built for entering an observation from the back of a classroom. Tablets get the full desktop version.

  • Pick a school and rubric once; it remembers them next time.
  • Teacher, date, course, domain scores, Glows and Grows, and the same Walkthrough toggle.
  • Autosaves as you go.
  • No email step.
  • Teacher-level rubrics only — school-wide scores go in from the cross-regional dashboard on a computer.
Chapter 5

Rescores

When a teacher-level walkthrough is submitted and the average across the scored domains comes out below 0.7, that teacher is flagged for a rescore, due three weeks from the walkthrough date.

Where it shows up

Action Center → Intervention → Rescore Queue, for that school. Intervention opens on Usage; Rescore Queue is next along and carries a count badge. Each row shows the teacher and the due date.

Who does it

Decided at the debrief — someone from Teaching & Learning, or the school’s own leader or coach.

How it clears

Observe the teacher again and submit it with the Walkthrough toggle on. An average of 0.7 or above clears the flag. Still below and the flag stays, with the clock reset to three weeks from the new date.

The toggle matters here too

A rescore only counts if it is marked as a walkthrough. Observe the teacher again with the toggle off and the queue will not budge, however well they did.
